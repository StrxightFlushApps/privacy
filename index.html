Privacy Policy

Leonidas Hub · Last updated: October 22, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app "Leonidas Hub" (the "App") handles your information. The App is an unofficial fan app for Grand Theft Auto VI. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Summary

The App does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The App saves a few settings locally on your device only, for example which map categories are shown. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App does not use analytics or tracking tools, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Content inside the App

The wiki texts and images are included in the App and work offline. To stay up to date, the App downloads the news feed from GitHub and map data from the community map map.gtadb.org. These requests only fetch content; like any web request, the server sees your IP address and basic device information, but the App sends no personal data. GitHub's and map.gtadb.org's own privacy policies apply to these requests.

External links

The App contains links to external websites, such as sources for wiki entries. When you open such a link, your browser connects to that website, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding advertising, we will update this page before the change goes live and adjust the date above.
