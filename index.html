Privacy Policy

Companion (working title) · Last updated: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app "Companion (working title)" (the "App") handles your information. The App is an unofficial fan app for Grand Theft Auto VI. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Summary

The App does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The App saves a few settings locally on your device only, for example whether sounds are on and which map categories are shown. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App does not use analytics or tracking tools, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Content inside the App

The map, wiki texts and images are included in the App and work offline. Showing them does not send any data to us or to third parties.

External links

The App contains links to external websites, such as sources for wiki entries. When you open such a link, your browser connects to that website, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding advertising, we will update this page before the change goes live and adjust the date above.
